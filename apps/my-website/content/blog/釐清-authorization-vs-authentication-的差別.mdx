## authentication

> the process of proving that something is real, true, or what people say it is:

換句說話就是一個可以證明你是真的程序。

也可以說是透過某種方式/方法來驗證

password authentication 密碼驗證

biometric authentication 生物驗證

fingerprint authentication 指紋辨識驗證

## authorization

### 解釋

> **1.** The act of authorizing.

> **2.** Something that authorizes; sanction.See Synonyms at permission

從中可以得知是一項得到授權的行為或任何獲得授權的事情

另一個字典的解釋

> official permission for something to happen, or the act of giving someone official permission to do something:

獲得允許去讓一件事情發生或是得到可以讓某事發生的行為

整段話就是授權的意思。

造句：children should get authorization by their parents to go to his friend’s house
